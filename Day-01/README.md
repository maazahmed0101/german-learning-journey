# 🇩🇪 German Learning — Day 01

Today I learned some basic German words and sentences related to **tea, coffee, milk, sugar, and polite expressions**.

---

## 📚 Vocabulary

| 🇩🇪 **German** | 🇬🇧 **English**        | 🗣️ **How to Speak** |
| --------------- | ----------------------- | -------------------- |
| **Tee**         | Tea                     | `Tay`                |
| **Kaffee**      | Coffee                  | `Ka-fe`              |
| **Zucker**      | Sugar                   | `Tsoo-ker`           |
| **Hallo**       | Hello                   | `Ha-lo`              |
| **Milch**       | Milk                    | `Milkh`              |
| **Danke**       | Thank you               | `Dan-keh`            |
| **Bitte**       | Please / You're welcome | `Bit-teh`            |
| **Mit**         | With                    | `Mit`                |
| **Und**         | And                     | `Oont`               |
| **Oder**        | Or                      | `Oh-der`             |

---

## 🗣️ Useful Sentences

### ☕ 1. Tee, bitte.

**English:** Tea, please.
**Speak:** `Tay, bit-teh`

---

### ☕ 2. Hallo, Tee oder Kaffee?

**English:** Hello, tea or coffee?
**Speak:** `Ha-lo, tay oh-der ka-fe`

---

### ☕ 3. Kaffee oder Tee?

**English:** Coffee or tea?
**Speak:** `Ka-fe oh-der tay`

---

### 🥛 4. Milch mit Zucker?

**English:** Milk with sugar?
**Speak:** `Milkh mit tsoo-ker`

---

### 🥛 5. Mit Milch.

**English:** With milk.
**Speak:** `Mit milkh`

---

### ☕ 6. Tee mit Milch, bitte.

**English:** Tea with milk, please.
**Speak:** `Tay mit milkh, bit-teh`

---
